there are multiple overarching types of quest. NPC quests, and key lock structure, there might be more that we havent found yet.

npc quests are the focus of the techreport, the npc has a motivation for the quest. These motivations are usualy one of these 9 knowledge, comfort, reputation, serenity, protection, conquest, wealth, ability and equipment.

the quest has a strategy, which is a very short description of what the quest is, usually 3-6 words, and it has a sequence of actions, which is an order of atomic actions that complete the quest.

an atomic action is a fundamental action that can be taken in the game, there are 20 descsribed in the paper, they can be seen in table 4 in the paper. the paper then goes on to mention that some actions can have rules which are a way to expand what an atomic action is for example we have the syntax "&lt;subquest&gt;" this could be expanded to "&lt;goto&gt; &lt;QUEST&gt; goto" this could mean you go somewhere complete a quest and then go back.

a quest then is made with an npc having a motivation for exaple &lt;comfort&gt; then she uses the a strategy "obtain luxuries" and then luxuries is broken down into atomic actions for the player to do for example "&lt;get&gt; &lt;goto&gt; &lt;give&gt;"

the paper then goes on to decsribe their own quest generator and a result from the generator making a quest.
