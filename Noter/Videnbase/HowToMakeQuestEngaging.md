# how to make a quest engaging

## Dramatic quest
you want a quest to be dramatic, drama is described in source 1 with this quote "DRAMA, AGAIN, IS THE QUEST OF THE HERO TO OVERCOME THOSE THINGS WHICH PREVENT HIM FROM ACHIEVING A SPECIFIC, *ACUTE* GOAL."
source 1 then goes on to describe a method to ensure drama, you go over three questions 1 who wants what 2 what happens if they dont get it 3 why now. answering these questions should tell you wether your quest is dramatic. 

## Not a chore
you want your quest to feel less like a chore and to make narrative progression, you want it to feel like you accomplished something
